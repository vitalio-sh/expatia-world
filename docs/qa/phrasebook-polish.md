The delivery lane uses a curated subset of the user-supplied Expat Meme Phrasebook. `src/shared/phrasebook.ts` exports Spanish recognition phrases with English translations and gentle coaching notes for onboarding, clarification, getting lost, waiting, and each physical outcome. Collection still explicitly requires collection; flavor never changes world state or promises a redelivery. Bureaucracy statistics, fees, floor numbering, real ID requests and unrelated scenarios were omitted.

Courier speech and text are now concise Castilian Spanish, while UI coaching stays English with optional bilingual recognition phrases. All courier fallback replies and optional joke cues are Spanish. Generic landmark descriptions and directions are translated; authored storefront proper names remain unchanged. The opening combines greeting, parcel and bar category in one sentence, then asks directions in a second, so the two-phrase speech limit preserves the question. One optional humane aside may accompany a useful answer when more than twenty seconds remain; opening and urgent directions have no joke cue. A successful physical receipt can have a brief celebratory cue. "Estoy abajo" is allowed only after validated physical entrance arrival. All three Scribe paths now autodetect language instead of forcing English.

The dialogue projection now separates current sight from observed memory, supports fruit/produce/greengrocer/frutería synonyms, building corners, street intersections, pedestrian crossings, current roads, and nearest/furthest visible facts. Building corners where walls turn stay distinct from junctions where streets meet. Memory-only entries have no live distance or direction. The speech guard rejects false denials of known semantic objects, falsely claiming remembered places are visible, fabricated metric conversions, and invented scenario distances. Look actions describe the new view; only a validated physical entrance arrival can announce readiness to accept the parcel. Meaningful regression coverage lives in `tests/dialogue.test.ts`.
